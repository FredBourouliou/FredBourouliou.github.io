## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters and potential partners landing on fredbourouliou.github.io from GitHub, LinkedIn or a CV. They want to understand in ~10 seconds who this is and see concrete, verifiable proof: live demos and source code.

### Brand Personality
Lettered, exact, unhurried. A former literature teacher (19 years) turned developer and instructional engineer: the page should read like an exhibition catalogue or a literary review's table of contents, confident without self-promotion.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial / catalogue. Paper-like light theme, ink-like dark theme, following the system setting. Numbered catalogue entries, asymmetric layout, one restrained accent (a red-ochre stamp colour). No gradients, no glow, no card grids, no badges.

### Design Principles
1. Proof over claims: every entry links to something that runs or to its source.
2. Typography carries the hierarchy; decoration stays minimal.
3. Asymmetry and rhythm instead of identical tiles.
4. Fast and static: one HTML file, one CSS file, WebP images, no JavaScript required.
5. Readable everywhere: WCAG AA contrast in both themes, reduced motion respected.
